Urdu / nastaliq OCR trained data goes here.

Download these two files and place them in THIS folder:
  - urd.traineddata
  - eng.traineddata
From: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best

(Use tessdata_best for the most accurate nastaliq recognition. The app copies
them to its private storage on first run. Without them, Urdu OCR will show a
"missing traineddata" message; English OCR via ML Kit still works.)
